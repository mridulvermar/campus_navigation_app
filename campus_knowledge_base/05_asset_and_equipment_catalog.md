## 3. Asset Checkout & Return Protocol
1. **Digital Reservation:** Reserve the asset in the mobile/web app selecting time slot and purpose.
2. **QR Pass Generation:** System generates an instant cryptographically signed QR Access Pass.
3. **Physical Handover:** Present QR pass at the asset's designated lab desk. Lab technician scans QR code to update status from `Reserved` to `In Use`.
4. **Inspection upon Return:** Technician verifies hardware condition, accessories, charging adapters, and lens cleanliness before checking the asset in as `Available`.
5. **Overdue Penalty:** Assets returned more than 30 minutes late without extension result in a 7-day reservation suspension.
