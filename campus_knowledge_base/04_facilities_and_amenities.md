# Bannari Amman Institute of Technology - Campus Facilities & Amenities Guide

This document covers all student living, healthcare, sports, dining, hospitality, and residential amenities on campus.

---

## 1. Residential Hostels
The campus provides separate on-campus accommodation with 24/7 security, WiFi, and modern amenities.

### A. BIT Boys Residential Hostel Complex (`BOYS-HST`)
- **Location:** South-East Sector (Coordinates: `11.494169, 77.278633`)
- **Capacity & Structure:** 6-story multi-wing complex housing 2,000+ students.
- **Room Types:** 2-sharing, 3-sharing, and 4-sharing rooms with attached & common bath options.
- **Dining:** 2 Large Vegetarian & Non-Vegetarian Mess Halls serving breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner.
- **Hostel Timings:** Entry curfew 09:30 PM (Biometric turnstile entry).
- **Amenities:** Indoor badminton court, table tennis, gym, 24/7 RO purified drinking water, hot water geysers, laundromat, reading room, study hall.
- **Warden Office Extension:** `6701`

### B. BIT Girls Residential Hostel Complex (`GIRLS-HST`)
- **Location:** South-West Sector (Coordinates: `11.493970, 77.275827`)
- **Capacity & Structure:** 5-story secure complex housing 1,800+ students.
- **Safety Features:** 24/7 dedicated female security staff, CCTV perimeter surveillance, RFID access gates.
- **Dining:** Dedicated dining hall with customized nutrition plans.
- **Hostel Timings:** Entry curfew 09:00 PM (Biometric logging).
- **Amenities:** Yoga & fitness studio, courtyard garden, TV lounge, convenience store, ATM kiosk, medical emergency first-aid station.
- **Warden Office Extension:** `6702`

## 2. Hospitality & Guest House (`GST-HOUSE`)
- **Location:** Near Gate A Main Entrance (Coordinates: `11.499858, 77.278559`)
- **Facilities:** 
  - 32 Deluxe Air-Conditioned VIP Suites with high-speed WiFi, smart TVs, and workspaces.
  - Dedicated Executive Dining Room offering continental, North Indian, and South Indian cuisines.
  - 24/7 front desk concierge and airport/railway pickup arrangement.
- **Booking Eligibility:** Faculty guests, visiting international researchers, recruiters, parents of students (subject to prior reservation).
- **Contact:** `guesthouse@campus.edu` / Ext `6105`.

## 3. Central Campus Cafeteria & Food Courts (`BIT-CAF`)
- **Location:** Central Sector (Coordinates: `11.493778, 77.277569`)
- **Hours:** 07:00 AM to 09:30 PM
- **Seating:** 1,200+ indoor air-conditioned and open-terrace seating.
- **Food Counters & Stalls:**
  1. *South Indian Kitchen:* Dosa, Idli, Pongal, Thali Meals, Biryani.
  2. *North Indian & Chinese Counter:* Roti, Paneer gravies, Noodles, Fried Rice, Rolls.
  3. *The Daily Grind Bakery & Cafe:* Fresh sandwiches, pastries, pizzas, cold coffee, espresso.
  4. *Organic Juice & Smoothie Bar:* Fresh seasonal fruit juices, shakes, tender coconut.
  5. *Snack Kiosks:* Chaat, samosas, tea, filter coffee.
- **Payment Options:** Campus Smart Card RFID, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking.

## 4. Healthcare & BIT Medical Center (`MED-CTR`)
- **Location:** West Sector near Gate C (Coordinates: `11.493860, 77.274593`)
- **Hours:** 24/7 Round-the-Clock Emergency Medical Care
- **Medical Staff:** 3 Full-Time Resident Medical Officers (MBBS), 6 Registered Nurses, On-call Specialists (Dentist, Orthopedic, Psychiatrist).
- **Facilities:**
  - 10-Bed Observation & Emergency Triage Ward.
  - 24/7 In-House Subsidized Pharmacy stocking essential and prescription medications.
  - Dedicated ICU Ambulance equipped with oxygen support, defibrillator, and ECG monitor.
- **Emergency Extension:** `6222` (Immediate ambulance dispatch: Ext `6223`).

## 6. Transportation & Vehicle Parking
- **Main Parking (`BIT-PRK`):** Accommodates 1,500 two-wheelers and 300 four-wheelers. EV charging points available.
- **Campus Fleet:** 65 GPS-tracked college buses connecting Erode, Coimbatore, Tirupur, Salem, Gobichettipalayam, and Sathyamangalam.
- **Internal E-Cart Shuttle:** Free electric shuttle carts run every 10 minutes connecting Gate A, Academic Blocks, Learning Center, and Hostels.
