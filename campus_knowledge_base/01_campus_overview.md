# Bannari Amman Institute of Technology (BIT) - Campus Overview & Core Information

## 1. Institution Identity & Campus Profile
- **Institution Name:** Bannari Amman Institute of Technology (BIT)
- **Campus Type:** Autonomous Engineering & Technology Institution, Multi-Discipline Research University Campus
- **Geographic Coordinates:** Latitude ~`11.4930` to `11.5005` N, Longitude ~`77.2735` to `77.2788` E
