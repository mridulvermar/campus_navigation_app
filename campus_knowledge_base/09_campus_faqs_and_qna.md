## 1. Navigation & Campus Wayfinding

**Q: Where is the Artificial Intelligence Lab located and how do I get there?**  
**A:** The Artificial Intelligence Lab (AI Lab) is located on the Ground Floor (Floor 1) of the SF Academic Block (`SF-BLK`), which houses the IT and Computer Science departments. You can enter the SF Block main lobby, proceed straight through the central corridor, and the AI Lab is situated on the left adjacent to Room IT 102.

**Q: How does turn-by-turn road navigation work in the campus app?**  
**A:** The navigation system uses a Dijkstra shortest-path routing algorithm operating on a graph of 320 calibrated campus road junctions. This ensures you are guided along legitimate paved roads, avenues, and sidewalks rather than cutting across building walls or lawns.

**Q: Where is the nearest parking lot to the SF Academic Block?**  
**A:** The nearest parking is the BIT Main Vehicle Parking (`BIT-PRK`), located just 150 meters north along the main avenue (Coordinates: 11.498394, 77.278229). It accommodates two-wheelers and four-wheelers and is equipped with EV charging stations.

**Q: How can I navigate from the Main Entrance Gate to the Vedhanayagam Auditorium?**  
**A:** From BIT Main Gate A (`BIT-GATE-A`), take the primary avenue heading south past the Aeronautical Block and SF Block. Turn right at the Central Clock Tower quadrangle onto the Auditorium Walkway. Vedhanayagam Auditorium (`VEDHA-AUD`) will be directly ahead on your right opposite the Learning Center. Walking time is approximately 6 minutes (~450 meters).

**Q: Is there an indoor floor map for classrooms in the IB Block?**  
**A:** Yes. The IB Block consists of two main wings: the Main Wing houses Classrooms 101-117 (Ground), 201-217 (1st Floor), and 301-317 (2nd Floor). The East Wing houses Classrooms 118-125, 218-225, and 318-325. Use the in-app floor toggle to switch levels.

**Q: Are there wheelchair-accessible routes across campus?**  
**A:** Yes. All academic blocks have ground-level entrance ramps. Elevators are operational in SF Block, IB Block, Learning Center, and AS Block. You can toggle "Wheelchair Accessible Route" in the navigation screen to filter out staircases.

## 2. Facility & Asset Booking

**Q: How do I book a computer lab or lecture hall?**  
**A:** Go to the "Facility Hub" or "Bookings" tab in the app. Select your desired building and room, choose the reservation date and time slot, enter the academic/project purpose, and tap "Confirm Booking". Once approved, an instant digital QR pass is generated.

**Q: What is the QR Code digital access pass and how do I use it?**  
**A:** The QR Code is a cryptographically signed digital pass generated for confirmed reservations. When arriving at your reserved room or lab, present the QR code to the optical door scanner or lab technician to check in and unlock access.

**Q: What happens if I am late for my reserved room or equipment booking?**  
**A:** The system enforces a 15-minute grace period. If you do not scan your QR pass to check in within 15 minutes of the scheduled start time, the booking is automatically cancelled as a "No-Show" and released to other users.

**Q: Can undergraduate students reserve high-end equipment like Apple MacBooks or VR Headsets?**  
**A:** Yes. Students can reserve Apple MacBook Pro M3 Max units (at Computer Center B-204) and Meta Quest 3 VR Development Headsets (at Innovation Lab A-102) for approved capstone projects and research tasks with faculty mentor endorsement.

**Q: How do I reserve the DJI Mavic 3 Pro Survey Drone?**  
**A:** The DJI Mavic 3 Pro drone is housed in the GIS Spatial Analytics Center (SF Block). Reservation requires submission at least 24 hours in advance and verification of drone pilot certification by the GIS Lab In-Charge.

**Q: How long can I reserve a 3D printer at the Maker Studio?**  
**A:** Ender 3 Pro 3D printing stations in Maker Studio B-105 (Mechanical Block) can be reserved from 2 hours up to 12 hours. Long overnight prints are permitted with lab technician authorization.

## 3. Campus Amenities, Dining & Hostels

**Q: What are the opening hours of the Central Learning Center (Library)?**  
**A:** The Central Learning Center (`LRN-CTR`) is open daily from 07:00 AM to 11:00 PM. During semester examinations, silent study halls remain open 24/7.

**Q: Where can I get food and what are the cafeteria operating hours?**  
**A:** The Central Campus Cafeteria (`BIT-CAF`) operates from 07:00 AM to 09:30 PM. It features South Indian, North Indian, Chinese food courts, a bakery, and a fresh organic juice bar. Digital payments via UPI and RFID campus cards are supported.

**Q: What are the hostel curfew and entry timings?**  
**A:** Boys Hostel (`BOYS-HST`) entry curfew is 09:30 PM. Girls Hostel (`GIRLS-HST`) entry curfew is 09:00 PM. Access is logged using biometric turnstile gates.

**Q: Can visitors or parents stay on campus?**  
**A:** Yes. Visitors, guest lecturers, and parents can stay at the BIT Guest House (`GST-HOUSE`) located near Main Gate A. It features 32 AC suites and an executive dining lounge. Bookings can be made via `guesthouse@campus.edu`.

## 4. Emergency, Healthcare & Safety

**Q: What is the campus emergency phone number?**  
**A:** For immediate emergency assistance, call internal extensions `6000` or `6111`, or external direct line `+91 (04295) 226000`. You can also trigger the Emergency SOS button in the app to broadcast your live GPS location to campus security.

**Q: Is there an ambulance on campus?**  
**A:** Yes. The BIT Medical Center (`MED-CTR`) maintains a dedicated ICU ambulance stationed 24/7. For urgent ambulance dispatch, call extension `6222` or `6223`.

**Q: Where is the BIT Medical Center located?**  
**A:** The Medical Center is located in the West Campus sector near Gate C (Coordinates: 11.493860, 77.274593). It has a 24/7 pharmacy, 10-bed observation ward, and resident medical officers.

## 5. Sports & Recreation

**Q: What sports facilities are available on campus?**  
**A:** Facilities include a 400m synthetic Athletic Track & Football Field (`ATHLETIC-CRT`), standard turf Cricket Ground (`CRICKET-GND`), floodlit synthetic Tennis Courts (`TENNIS-CRT`), Handball Court (`HANDBALL-CRT`), outdoor Basketball & Volleyball Sports Arena (`SPORTS-ARNA`), and an Indoor Badminton Complex (`BADMINTON-CRT`).

**Q: What are the operating hours for the sports complex and tennis courts?**  
**A:** Outdoor sports courts are open from 06:00 AM to 08:30 PM (Sports Arena open till 09:30 PM with floodlights). The synthetic athletic track is open from 05:30 AM to 09:00 PM.
