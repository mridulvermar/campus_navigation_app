> **Floor Numbering Standard**: For all classrooms and academic venues with 3-digit identifiers (e.g., `EW 102`, `EW 208`, `WW 102`, `CS 201`, `IT 001`, `ME 301`), the **first digit strictly designates the floor level**:
> • **0xx** = Ground Floor (Floor 0)
> • **1xx** = 1st Floor (Floor 1)
> • **2xx** = 2nd Floor (Floor 2)
> • **3xx** = 3rd Floor (Floor 3)
