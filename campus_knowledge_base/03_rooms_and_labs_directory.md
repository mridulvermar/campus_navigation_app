# Bannari Amman Institute of Technology - Rooms, Laboratories & Academic Spaces Directory

This document details the indexed academic spaces across all major blocks, including room number conventions, capacities, equipment/facilities, and floor-by-floor organization for 428 campus rooms and specialized laboratories.

---

## 1. Room Numbering Convention
Campus room numbers follow a standardized indexing pattern:
- **SF Block (Self-Financing):** Prefix `IT` (e.g., `IT 001` = Base Floor, `IT 101` = Ground Floor, `IT 201` = 1st Floor, `IT 301` = 2nd Floor).
- **IB Block (Institution Building):** 
  - Main Wing: Classes `101` to `117` (Floor 1/Ground), `201` to `217` (Floor 2), `301` to `317` (Floor 3).
  - East Wing: Classes `118` to `125` (Floor 1/Ground), `218` to `225` (Floor 2), `318` to `325` (Floor 3).
- **AS Block (Applied Science):** Classes `AS 101` to `AS 110` (Floor 1), `AS 201` to `AS 210` (Floor 2), `AS 301` to `AS 310` (Floor 3).
- **Aeronautical Block:** `AERO 101` to `AERO 108`, Aerodynamics & Propulsion Labs.
- **Mechanical Block:** `MECH 101` to `MECH 112`, CNC, Thermal & Robotics Labs.
- **Learning Center:** `LIB 101` (Digital Hub), `LIB 201` (Research Pod), `LIB 301` (Audio Visual Room).
