## 3. Lost & Found Management Hub
- **Physical Desk:** Main Security Office near Gate A (`BIT-GATE-A`) & Admin Helpdesk.
- **App Module:** Users can browse lost items or report discovered belongings (wallets, smartwatches, ID cards, lab aprons, calculation tools).
- **Claim Process:** Bring proof of identity or matching description to the Security Desk for verification.
